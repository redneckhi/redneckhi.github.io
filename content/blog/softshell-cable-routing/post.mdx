## Goal

Pass a radio lead from hip pouch to chest mic without fighting the zipper every time you sit down.

## Pattern

1. Exit the softshell at the side seam, not through the collar
2. Use a short internal sleeve so the cable does not saw the laminate
3. Leave a service loop at the shoulder — enough slack to raise an arm

## Materials

| Piece | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| #5 coil zipper | Water-resistant, not waterproof theater |
| Flat cable | Round coax chafe more |
| Heat-shrink collar | Stops the grommet from walking |

## Mistakes we already made

Routing under the high-vis tape looked clever until the tape lifted. Keep cable paths separate from branding panels.

## Checklist

- Lead seated with arm raised and lowered
- Drain path still clear at the hem
- No hard bend at the radio connector
