## The Idea

As anything we produce here at RHI, our ~~product~~ specimen naming scheme sprung from a convoluted laying-awake-at-3am thought:

Every specimen has it's own number designation indicating what type of prototype it is as well as a prototype codename.

## Overthinking Numbers

So lets' get into the meat of this mess.

A specimen designation consists of a letter as well as it identifiying number, for example:

`S-311`

Yes, we could have just called things "Doohickey V2" like the ~~normal people~~ professionals at ~~REDACTED~~ Concepts or ~~REDACTED~~ Dynamics. Anyways...

Breaking `S-311` down:

| Part | In the example | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Letter | `S` | General category (software, tool, clothing, misc.) |
| 1st digit | `3` | Narrow category inside that letter |
| 2nd digit | `1` | Individual ID within that narrow category |
| 3rd digit | `1` | Revision number (after reaching PRODUCTION stage) |

So left-to-right you go from "what kind of thing is this" to "which one is it" to "how many times have we already broken this one in prod."

If you catch yourself arguing about whether something is a `T` or an `M` for twenty minutes: congratulations, you are doing RHI correctly. 

## The numbers, Jason! What do they mean?

Here's a handy cheat-sheet for when your brain refuses to store this permanently (ours doesn't either):

| Letter designation | Description |
|---|---|
| S | Software |
| T | Tool |
| C | Clothing |
| M | Misc. |

| Letter designation | Narrow Category Number | Description |
|---|---|---|
| S | 1 | Setup/Automation |
| S | 2 | Networking |
| S | 3 | AI |
| S | 4-9 | Reserved for future use |
| T | 1 | Pouches/LBE |
| T | 2 | Organizer |
| T | 3 | Attachments |
| T | 4-9 | Reserved for future use |
| C | 1-9 | Reserved for future use |
| M | 1-9 | Reserved for future use |

"Reserved for future use" is corporate for "we will absolutely invent a new niche at 2am and refuse to renumber everything."

The narrow categories are deliberately coarse. If a pouch also holds a radio and your feelings, it is still a pouch until it graduates into something we can defend with a straight face. Misc. exists for the orphans: the one-off jigs, cursed cables, and "this solved a problem once and now it has a SKU."
