## Setup

We ran three cheap SBCs and two handsets on a ridge trail for 36 hours. Rain was intermittent. Power was scavenged USB banks. Nobody called it a test plan; we called it a walk.

## What broke

### Antenna routing

Cable chafe at the softshell exit ate one coax. Tape helps once. A grommet helps twice. Plan for the chafe before the weather does.

### Duplicate floods

When nodes reappeared after a blackout, the mesh re-announced everything it had cached. Fine on paper. Loud on a 9600 baud link. Cap gossip bursts or you will burn duty cycle explaining old news.

### Time sync

Wall clocks drifted. Packet timestamps without a shared epoch made “latest” meaningless. We now seed with GPS when available and fall back to “good enough relative order” when not.

## What we kept

- Prefer delivery over elegance
- Inspectable packets over clever compression
- A written PSK rotation note that someone actually reads

## Next season

Bring spare coax, a drain plan for pouches, and one person whose only job is watching the LED that means “still alive.”
