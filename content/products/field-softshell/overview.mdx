The **Field Softshell Mk.I** is our first clothing prototype: a work jacket that treats cable management and visibility as first-class features, not afterthoughts.

## What you get

- DWR-treated face fabric that shrugs off drizzle
- High-vis tape shoulders for night ops near vehicles
- Internal channel for radio coil / earpiece routing
- Zippered chest pocket sized for a field notebook
