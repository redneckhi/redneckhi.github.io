The **Torque Bit Field Kit** is a BOM and foam layout for the driver bits that show up in real RHI builds — rack screws, radio chassis, MOLLE hardware, and softshell zippers.

## Contents philosophy

- Only bits that earned a slot after three field seasons
- Foam cutout so you notice what is missing
- Magnetized rail for the three bits you always lose

## Not included

Novelty multitools, decorative titanium nonsense, or bits for appliances you do not own.
