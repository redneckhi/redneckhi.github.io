## Overview

This document is the build-your-own kit list for the Torque Bit Field Kit. Buy a tin, cut foam, fill it once, and stop scavenging bits from desk drawers before a deployment.

## Container

- Tin or hard case ≈ 180 × 100 × 35 mm
- Closed-cell foam, 10–15 mm, knife-cut
- Optional: laser-cut acrylic divider for magnets

## Bit list

| Hex 2.5 | 1 | camera mounts |

| Hex 4 | 1 | heavier mounts |

### Fasteners pouch

Carry a small zipper bag of M3/M4 screws, lock washers, and Chicago screws for webbing. Restock after every build weekend.

## Foam layout

![Torque Bit Field Kit tin layout](/products/torque-bit-kit/01.svg)

1. Trace bits on paper, transfer to foam
2. Cut slightly undersize so bits stay put when the tin is inverted
3. Leave one empty slot labeled `LOST` — honesty helps inventory

## Torque notes

Hand-tight plus a quarter turn is not a torque spec. For radio chassis, use a small torque screwdriver where the OEM publishes values. Stripped threads in the field are not a personality trait.

## Maintenance

Wipe bits after wet days. Replace rounded bits immediately — they destroy fasteners faster than they save weight.

## Packing checklist

- Bits seated
- Magnet rail loaded with PH2 / T10 / Hex 3
- Fastener pouch closed
- Tin latch functional
