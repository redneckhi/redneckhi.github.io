The **Admin Dump Pouch Mk.II** is a MOLLE-compatible open-top pouch for spent mags, gloves, and the miscellaneous junk that accumulates mid-mission.

## Features

- Stiffened rim that stays open with one hand
- Bottom drain grommet
- Flat admin sleeve on the face for cards / light sticks
- Collapses flatter when empty than Mk.I

## Materials

Cordura-class nylon, binding tape, plastic stiffener, and webbing you can buy by the meter. Pattern included in docs.
