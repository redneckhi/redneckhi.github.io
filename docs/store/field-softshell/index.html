Overview

The Field Softshell Mk.I is a reproducible pattern and bill of materials for a tactical softshell. Redneck Heavy Industries ships the design intent; you cut, sew, and revise for your climate.

Design goals

  • Stay dry in light rain without becoming a sauna
  • Keep high-vis panels where vehicle crews will see you
  • Route a radio cable without snagging on pack straps
  • Use off-the-shelf fabrics you can reorder next season

Bill of materials

Fabrics

  • Softshell face: 3-layer stretch softshell, ~280–320 gsm
  • High-vis tape: 50 mm retroreflective / warning-tape yellow
  • Zippers: #5 coil, water-resistant preferred
  • Elastic: 25 mm for hem and cuff options

Hardware

  • Cord locks (2)
  • Snap fasteners for storm flap (optional)
  • Bias tape or binding for raw edges

Pattern notes

Blocks

  1. Front left / front right with zipper allowance
  2. Back panel with shoulder yoke
  3. Sleeve with underarm gusset
  4. Hood or collar (Mk.I ships with stand collar)

High-vis placement

Apply tape across the upper back and both shoulder caps. Keep tape continuous across seams where possible so night visibility does not break at stitch lines.

Assembly sequence

Prep

  1. Pre-wash fabrics if the supplier recommends it
  2. Cut all panels; mark cable channel on left chest
  3. Fuse or baste reflective tape before final stitching

Body

  1. Join shoulder seams
  2. Set sleeves
  3. Side seams and underarm continuous seam
  4. Install main zipper and storm flap

Cable channel

Sew a narrow tunnel from hem of collar to left chest pocket. Leave a 12 mm exit eyelet lined with binding. Test with a radio coil before closing the lining.

Care

  • Cold wash, hang dry
  • Do not iron reflective tape
  • Re-apply DWR when water stops beading

Field revisions

Log failures. If a seam blows or a panel snags, note size, climate, and loadout. Mk.II will fold those notes into the pattern. Good enough means we ship, then we harden.
